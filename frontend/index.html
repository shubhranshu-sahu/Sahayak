How It Works

From Zero to Fully Managed
in 5 Simple Steps

Setting up your society on Sahayak takes minutes, not weeks.

01

Secretary Signs Up

The society secretary creates an account with their name, email, and phone. Verified instantly — no waiting, no approvals needed.

02

Build Your Structure

Use the guided setup wizard to define your society — add blocks (A, B, C...), floors per block, and units per floor. Bulk creation saves hours.

03

Residents Join

Residents find their society, select their exact unit from cascading dropdowns (Block → Floor → Unit), and submit a registration request.

04

Secretary Approves

The secretary reviews pending requests on their dashboard. One click to approve or reject — the unit status updates automatically.

05

AI Takes Over

Sahayak AI becomes your digital secretary — it answers resident queries, drafts notices, tracks complaints, and learns your society's context.

Industry-standard JWT tokens with role-based access. Secretaries and residents see only what they need to.

Hierarchical Structure

Society → Blocks → Floors → Units. The entire physical structure modeled digitally with auto-generated labels like A-101.
